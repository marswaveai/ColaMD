最新一版加了标签页。

写文章的时候，要用的素材往往是另一份文件。以前得先关掉手上这份，再打开那份，看一眼又换回来。现在在文件列表里按住 `⌘` 点一下名字，它就在新标签里开着，两边都能看，随手对照，写完了点掉就行。只开着一份文档的时候，标签栏根本不出现。

ColaMD 是一块画布，你的文字才是主角。

## English

**ColaMD is a free, elegant Markdown editor: you open it and see your text and a file list, no toolbar, and your files stay on your own computer, saved as you write.**

The latest release adds tabs.

When you write, the material you need is usually another file. Before, you had to close what you were writing, open that file, take a look, then switch back. Now hold `⌘` and click the name in the file list: it opens in a new tab, both stay put, and you can glance across whenever you want. Close the tab when you are done. With a single document open, the tab strip does not appear at all.

ColaMD is a canvas, and your words are the main thing.
