# ColaMD v2.1.1 Social Copy

## Chinese

ColaMD 2.1.1 已发布，这一版都在打磨标签页的手感。

- **加号回到标签栏右端**：有标签栏的时候，新建标签页的加号就紧跟最后一个标签，跟浏览器一样顺手。标题栏不再放加号
- **标签上可以右键了**：关闭、关闭其他标签页、关闭右侧标签页，以及复制路径、在 Finder 或资源管理器中显示
- **悬停提示更安静**：要停够一秒才出现，而且只显示 `⌘W`；只有名字长到被截断时才把完整名字带出来

## English

ColaMD 2.1.1 is out: a small release that polishes the tab strip.

- **The plus is back at the end of the strip**: with tabs open, the new-tab button sits right after the last one, the way a browser does it. The title bar no longer carries a plus
- **Tabs have a right-click menu**: close, close other tabs, close tabs to the right, copy path, and reveal in Finder or File Explorer
- **A quieter hover hint**: it waits a second before it appears and shows only `⌘W`; a name long enough to be truncated is spelled out in full

Free, open source, local first, on macOS, Windows and Linux. The app will offer the update by itself:
