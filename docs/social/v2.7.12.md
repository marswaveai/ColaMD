# ColaMD v2.7.12 Social Copy

发布方式：正式版。

## 主文案（直接发）

ColaMD 2.7.12 发布，集中修复列表输入手感与长文档复制卡顿。

- 退出列表后直接打字保持顶格，不再被缩进成列表续行。
- 敲 `1.` 还没按空格时不提前改号或缩进，隔着普通段落另起列表从 `1.` 重新计数。
- 列表首字前按退格直接退缩进或拆掉标记，不再留下多余空格；待办框后退格只拆方框。
- 引用里的列表支持 Tab 缩进、退格拆解与点击勾选待办，空引用行按一次回车直接退出。
- 长文档拖选与复制不再重算整篇文档，消除选字跳变与复制卡顿。
- 图片加载完成后自动更新块高，下方文字点击落点更准。

https://github.com/marswaveai/ColaMD/releases/tag/v2.7.12

## English

ColaMD 2.7.12 is out, fixing list editing edge cases and copy lag in long documents.

- Typing on a plain line after exiting a list stays flush left instead of indenting as a continuation line.
- Typing `1.` keeps the text as-is until a space is entered, and a new ordered list after a plain paragraph starts back at `1.`.
- Backspace before the first character of a list item removes the checkbox, outdents one level, or strips the marker cleanly without leaving spaces behind.
- Lists nested inside blockquotes now support Tab indent, Backspace unwrap, and task checkbox clicks inside `> `, and pressing Enter once exits an empty quote line.
- Drag-selecting and copying visible text in long documents now runs synchronously without rebuilding document-wide decorations (#201).
- Loaded images trigger a block height re-measure so clicks below images land on the right line.

https://github.com/marswaveai/ColaMD/releases/tag/v2.7.12
